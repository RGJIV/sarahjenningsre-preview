Mountain Real Estate, With a Local Perspective

Buying or selling a home in Cashiers and the surrounding mountains is about more than square footage, a view, or a price per square foot. It's about understanding the communities, the roads, the seasons, the amenities, and the details that can make a real difference in a mountain property.

I've spent years living and working on the Cashiers-Highlands Plateau, and I bring that local knowledge to every client I work with. My approach is straightforward: listen carefully, know the market, present properties thoughtfully, and give you honest advice—even when it isn't necessarily what you want to hear.

Whether you're looking for a mountain retreat, a family home, an investment property, or preparing to sell a property you've loved for years, I'm here to make the process feel informed, personal, and manageable.

I know the mountain. I know the market. And I'm here to help you make the right move.
